BAO CAO NANG CAP CHUC NANG ADMIN - 03/06/2026

Pham vi:
- Nang cap 10 nhom chuc nang admin da de xuat.
- Se push len GitHub sau khi kiem tra lint/build theo yeu cau moi.

1. Dang nhap admin owner
- Project chi co mot nguoi quan tri nen dua ve 1 role duy nhat: owner.
- Admin session tra ve role owner, expiresAt va csrfToken.
- Owner co toan quyen quan tri, khong can giai thich nhieu vai tro nhu admin/moderator/support.
- Trang tong quan hien thong tin phien admin: email, vai tro chu he thong, thoi han phien.

2. Bao mat thao tac admin
- Them CSRF token rieng cho admin session.
- Tat ca thao tac ghi quan trong gui header x-admin-csrf.
- Backend chan POST/PATCH/DELETE neu thieu CSRF hop le.
- Cac thao tac nguy hiem co xac nhan tren frontend: huy tran, ket thuc tran, huy giai, ket thuc giai.

3. Toi uu tai du lieu admin
- AdminPage khong con goi hang loat API cua moi tab khi moi vao.
- Moi tab chi tai API cua chinh tab do, giam loi 401/403/500 hang loat khi phien admin het han.
- Nut lam moi chi lam moi du lieu cua tab dang mo.

4. Quan ly giai dau
- Giai dau co thao tac cap nhat trang thai: mo dang ky, bat dau, ket thuc, huy giai.
- Backend co PATCH /api/admin/tournaments va ghi audit log.
- Danh sach giai dau hien tong nguoi tham gia, tong so van da ghi nhan, top 1-2-3.
- Tao giai dau van gui thong bao den nguoi choi nhu truoc.

5. Quan ly tran dau
- Tran dau co thao tac admin: huy/danh dau bo van, ket thuc hoa.
- Backend co PATCH /api/admin/matches va ghi audit log.
- Danh sach tran hien ma moi, trang thai rated, ve ghep tran, so nuoc va canh bao tran treo qua 15 phut.

6. Thong bao nguoi choi
- Luong tao giai dau tiep tuc tao notification cho nguoi choi.
- Noi dung thong bao giai dau moi dieu huong ve /tournaments va nhac canh tranh top 1-2-3.

7. Anti-cheat va fair play
- API anti-cheat duoc bao ve bang quyen fairplay:manage va CSRF cho thao tac cap nhat.
- Quet anti-cheat cua admin cung yeu cau quyen va CSRF.
- Trang tong quan hien so nguoi choi rui ro cao va bao cao dang cho xu ly.

8. Bao cao nguoi choi / kiem duyet
- API moderation duoc bao ve bang quyen moderation:manage va CSRF cho thao tac xu ly.
- Danh sach bao cao hien canh bao neu trong trang hien tai co nhieu bao cao cung mot nguoi bi report.
- Tiep tuc ho tro cac trang thai: dang xem, chuyen cap cao hon, da xu ly, bo qua.

9. Ho so nguoi choi cho admin
- Chi tiet nguoi choi bo sung:
  + Bao cao nguoi choi lien quan.
  + Su kien hoan diem fair play.
  + Trang thai goi thanh vien.
  + So bao cao dang cho xu ly.
- Modal chi tiet giu dang tom tat de admin doc nhanh.

10. Nhat ky quan tri
- Audit log co them nhan de doc cho:
  + match.update_status
  + tournament.update_status
- Trang audit van phan trang theo URL va khong tai danh sach dai mot lan.

11. Light mode / dark mode va localStorage
- Giu cac sua doi truoc do: mac dinh dark mode de tranh nen sang lam mat chu.
- Light mode co rule tang do tuong phan cho text/input.
- Khong con localStorage trong Frontend/src va backend/src.

12. Giai dau cho nguoi choi
- Tach luong giai dau cong khai thanh danh sach va trang chi tiet theo URL.
- Nguoi choi co the tham gia, roi giai khi giai chua bat dau, xem top 1-2-3 va bang xep hang day du.
- Khi 2 nguoi cung tham gia mot giai va choi tran online dung time control trong thoi gian giai, ket qua duoc ghi vao arena_tournament_games va diem BXH tu cap nhat.
- Moi van trong giai co lich su gan day de mo lai Game Review.

13. Lich su tran dau
- Lich su online duoc sua lai tieng Viet hien thi cho dung.
- Bo loc ket qua, che do va trang thai review di theo URL.
- Phan trang lich su tinh theo API, co the nhap truc tiep page/result/mode/review tren URL.
